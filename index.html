Meet your new kitchen routine

College Eats was created by fictional college student Mia Bennett, who wanted a better way to plan meals around a student budget. Her approach is simple: choose a few versatile ingredients, keep recipes manageable, and make a plan before shopping.

This week's student tip: Before buying groceries, check what you already have. Half a bag of rice, a few eggs, and frozen vegetables can become a meal instead of forgotten ingredients at the back of a cupboard.

Start with a few pantry basics

Rice, oats, pasta, canned beans, eggs, peanut butter, canned tomatoes, and frozen vegetables are useful starting points. You do not need a fully stocked kitchen or expensive specialty ingredients to put together satisfying meals.

Recipe prices throughout this fictional website are estimates for educational purposes. Actual costs depend on location, store, package size, and ingredients already on hand.
