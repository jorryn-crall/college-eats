Meet your new kitchen routine

College Eats was created around a simple idea: students should be able to plan practical meals without spending too much or making cooking unnecessarily complicated. Choose versatile ingredients, keep recipes manageable, and make a plan before shopping.

This week's student tip: Before buying groceries, check what you already have. Rice, eggs, and frozen vegetables can become a meal instead of forgotten ingredients at the back of a cupboard.

Start with a few pantry basics

Rice, oats, pasta, eggs, peanut butter, canned tomatoes, and frozen vegetables are useful starting points. You do not need a fully stocked kitchen or expensive specialty ingredients to put together satisfying meals.

Recipe prices throughout this fictional website are estimates for educational purposes. Actual costs depend on location, store, package size, and ingredients already on hand.
