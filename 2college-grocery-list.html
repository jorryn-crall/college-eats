The Ultimate College Grocery List

Shopping for groceries on a college budget does not have to be complicated. College Eats helps you stock your kitchen with affordable, versatile ingredients you can use for multiple meals.

College Grocery List Essentials

Start with these budget-friendly staples. Choose the items you enjoy, check what you already have, and build your list around the meals you plan to make.

Protein

  • Eggs
  • Canned tuna or chicken
  • Chicken breast or thighs
  • Greek yogurt
  • Peanut butter
  • Canned beans or chickpeas

Grains and Carbs

  • Rice
  • Pasta
  • Oatmeal
  • Bread
  • Potatoes
  • Tortillas or wraps

Fruits and Vegetables

  • Bananas
  • Apples
  • Frozen mixed vegetables
  • Spinach
  • Carrots
  • Canned tomatoes

Pantry and Dairy

  • Milk or a milk alternative
  • Shredded cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Marinara sauce

  • Protein: $12
  • Grains and carbs: $8
  • Fruits and vegetables: $12
  • Pantry and dairy: $13

Estimated total: $45 per week

5 Tips for Saving Money on Groceries

  1. Plan before you shop. Choose a few meals for the week and write down only the ingredients you need.
  2. Buy versatile ingredients. Rice, eggs, pasta, and frozen vegetables can be used in several different meals.
  3. Compare unit prices. Check the price per ounce or pound to compare package sizes.
  4. Use frozen and canned produce. These options can be affordable, convenient, and easier to store than fresh produce.
  5. Reduce food waste. Plan meals that reuse ingredients you already purchased.
