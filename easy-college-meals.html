Easy College Meals for Your Busiest Weeks

When classes, assignments, work, and campus activities fill your schedule, cooking can feel overwhelming. These easy college meals use familiar ingredients and straightforward instructions, so you can spend less time figuring out dinner and more time enjoying it.

Each idea includes an estimated preparation time and the equipment you need. Choose recipes that fit your kitchen, whether you have a stove or just a microwave.

4 Easy Meals to Make This Week

1. Microwave Egg Mug

5 minutes · Microwave · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Whisk the eggs and milk in a large microwave-safe mug or bowl.
  2. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring between intervals, until the eggs are fully cooked.
  3. Stir in the cheese and season to taste.

Easy upgrade: Add leftover vegetables or serve with toast.

2. Peanut Butter Banana Oats

5 minutes · Microwave · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 3/4 cup milk or water
  • 1/2 banana, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • A pinch of cinnamon

Directions

  1. Combine oats and liquid in a large microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Microwave according to the oat package directions, watching for overflow.
  3. Top with banana, peanut butter, and cinnamon.

Easy upgrade: Add a little honey if you prefer extra sweetness.

3. Chicken and Rice Bowl

10 minutes · Microwave · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 3 ounces fully cooked chicken
  • 1/2 cup frozen vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon teriyaki sauce

Directions

  1. Put the rice, chicken, and vegetables in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Add a splash of water and cover loosely.
  3. Microwave until everything is steaming hot, stirring halfway through. Follow package directions for reheating the chicken.
  4. Stir in teriyaki sauce and serve.

Easy upgrade: Use leftover cooked chicken to save time.

4. Pesto Grilled Cheese

10 minutes · Skillet · 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 2 slices cheese
  • 1 teaspoon pesto
  • 1 teaspoon butter

Directions

  1. Spread pesto on one slice of bread and add the cheese.
  2. Close the sandwich and butter the outside of both bread slices.
  3. Cook in a skillet over medium-low heat until both sides are golden and the cheese has melted.

Easy upgrade: Pair with tomato soup for a more filling meal.

Cooking With Limited Kitchen Equipment

If You Have a Microwave

Try oatmeal, microwave egg mugs, baked potatoes, steamed frozen vegetables, and rice bowls. Use microwave-safe dishes and follow the cooking instructions for each ingredient.

If You Have a Stove

Make pasta, quesadilla-style flatbreads, scrambled eggs, skillet rice, and grilled cheese. A single skillet and one pot can handle many basic recipes.

The one-pan rule: On a busy night, choose a recipe that uses one pan or bowl. Less cleanup makes it easier to cook at home consistently.

Build Your Own Easy College Meal

Start with one base, add a protein, include a fruit or vegetable, and finish with a sauce or seasoning you enjoy.

  • Base: Rice, pasta, oats, bread, or potatoes.
  • Protein: Eggs, chicken, cheese, yogurt, or canned chickpeas.
  • Produce: Frozen vegetables, spinach, bananas, or apples.
  • Flavor: Pesto, salsa, soy sauce, garlic powder, or Italian seasoning.

Mix and match these ingredients to create meals that suit your preferences and budget without needing a complicated recipe.
